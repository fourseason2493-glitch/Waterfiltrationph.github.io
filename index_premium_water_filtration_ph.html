01 • THE SOLUTION

Our Water Filtration System

Our wall-mounted alkaline water filtration system is designed around a 6-stage filtration process to support convenient access to filtered drinking water at the point of use.

1

Sediment Filter

Helps reduce suspended particles such as dirt, sand, and rust.

2

Pre-Filtration

Provides additional particulate protection.

3

Carbon Filtration

Helps reduce chlorine and unwanted taste and odor.

4

Fine Filtration

Provides additional filtration of selected fine particles.

5

Mineral / Media Stage

Supports the intended water-conditioning process.

6

Alkaline Filter

Raises the pH according to the system design and provides the system's alkaline-water stage.
